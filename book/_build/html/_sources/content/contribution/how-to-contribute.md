# How to contribute

Contributions from researchers, practitioners, and students are welcome. The project aims to reflect the diversity of experimental practices across laboratories while encouraging clearer documentation and reporting standards.

Possible contributions include:

- suggestions for additional physiological variables or derived metrics
- clarifications of terminology or symbolic notation
- methodological notes on measurement protocols or instrumentation
- examples of reporting practices or metadata schemas
- corrections or updates based on recent literature

Contributions can typically be made by submitting a pull request, opening an issue in the repository, or contacting the editorial team directly. Smaller clarifications and corrections are especially encouraged, as these often improve usability for the broader community.
