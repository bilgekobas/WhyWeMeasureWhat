# Citation, versioning, and updates

This resource is maintained as a living methodological reference and may be periodically updated as the field develops.

Updates may include:

- corrections,
- expanded methodological guidance,
- revised terminology,
- additional references,
- metadata schema extensions,
- or new methodological sections.

To support transparency and reproducibility, all major changes are tracked through the public project repository:

https://github.com/bilgekobas/WhyWeMeasureWhat

Major releases are archived through Zenodo and assigned version-specific DOIs so that readers can reference stable snapshots of the resource used in their work.

## How to cite

**Peer-reviewed framework paper**

> Kobas, B. (2026). *Toward Interoperable Indoor Thermal Physiology Research in the Built Environment: An Interpretive Framework for Physiological Measurement and Metadata Architecture*. *Building and Environment*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.buildenv.2026.115269

**Versioned online resource**

For the scientific framework, cite the peer-reviewed paper above. When using particular wiki content, templates, or a specific version of this evolving resource, also cite the archived Zenodo release corresponding to the version used during analysis or manuscript preparation.

Recommended citation format:

> Kobas, B. (2026). *Why We Measure What, How and Where: Methods for Human Thermal Physiology Research in the Built Environment* (Version 0.1.0). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20073568

Because the resource is periodically updated, including either the version number or the access date is strongly recommended.

Example:

> Accessed Version 0.1.0 (2026-05-07)

or

> Accessed 7 May 2026 from https://bilgekobas.github.io/WhyWeMeasureWhat/

When referencing specific methodological concepts or formula collections, readers are encouraged to cite both the overall resource and the relevant section references where appropriate.
