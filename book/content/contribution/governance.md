# Governance

The document follows a lightweight editorial governance model intended to balance openness with quality control. Major structural changes and methodological recommendations are reviewed by the editors before being incorporated into the main version of the document.

Proposed additions or revisions may be submitted through the project repository or by contacting the editorial team. Submissions are evaluated based on several criteria:

- scientific validity and methodological clarity
- consistency with existing terminology and conventions
- relevance for researchers conducting thermal physiology experiments
- transparency and reproducibility of the described methods

Where appropriate, revisions are discussed publicly within the project repository to allow community feedback before adoption.
