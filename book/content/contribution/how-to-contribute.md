# How to contribute

Contributions from researchers, practitioners, students, and interdisciplinary collaborators are warmly welcome.

The project aims to reflect the diversity of experimental practices, analytical approaches, and tacit methodological knowledge across laboratories and disciplines while supporting more transparent and interpretable physiological research practices.

Contributions may include:

- suggestions for additional physiological variables or derived metrics,
- clarification of terminology, definitions, or interpretations,
- methodological notes on instrumentation or measurement protocols,
- examples of reporting practices or metadata schemas,
- corrections or updates informed by recent literature,
- technical improvements to the repository or website,
- or other additions that improve clarity, comparability, and within-field communication.

Contributions can typically be made by:

- opening an issue in the repository,
- submitting a pull request,
- proposing corrections or additions directly,
- or contacting the project editor.

If you are unsure how best to contribute, opening a discussion through an issue is usually a good starting point.

All contributions are reviewed and integrated with care to maintain consistency across the resource. Smaller clarifications and corrections are especially valuable and often substantially improve usability for other researchers.
