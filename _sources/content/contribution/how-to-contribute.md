# How to contribute

Contributions from researchers, practitioners, and students are very welcome. The project aims to reflect the diversity of experimental practices and tacit knowledge across laboratories, while gradually building a more consistent and transparent way of documenting and reporting physiological data.

You are encouraged to contribute at any level, from small clarifications to more substantial additions. These may include:

- suggestions for additional physiological variables or derived metrics,
- clarifications of terminology, definitions, explanations, or interpretations,
- methodological notes on measurement protocols or instrumentation, including practice-based observations from lab or field work,
- examples of reporting practices, metadata schemas, or lab-specific standardisation approaches,
- corrections or updates informed by recent literature,
- or anything else that helps improve clarity, comparability, and within-field communication.

Contributions can typically be made by submitting a pull request, opening an issue in the repository, or contacting the editorial team directly. If you are unsure how best to contribute, starting a discussion via an issue is always a good first step.

All contributions are reviewed and integrated with care to maintain consistency across the document. Where appropriate, contributions are attributed so that contributors receive credit for their input.

Smaller clarifications and corrections are especially valuable — they often make a significant difference in how usable the resource is for others.
