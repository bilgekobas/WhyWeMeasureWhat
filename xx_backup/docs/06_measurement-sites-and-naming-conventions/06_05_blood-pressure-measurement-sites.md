# BLOOD PRESSURE MEASUREMENT SITES




This appendix compiles the anatomical sites used for skin-temperature measurement, standardised into 19 body regions. Each site may include left/right or anterior/posterior counterparts; these are only specified when relevant.\

Letters A–U from the previous chapter, [MEAN SKIN TEMPERATURE FORMULAS](#mean-skin-temperature-formulas), are retained solely to map historical formulas to the standardised regions shown in Table 8 and Figure 2.
